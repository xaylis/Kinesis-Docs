import { Card, CardGrid } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Model materials" icon="seti:image">
    Grow a mob back from nothing, dissolve it into embers, turn it to stone or into a hologram. Masks travel from a
    point, along an axis or out along the skeleton, with layered surfaces behind the front.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Effects are files" icon="document">
    Describe an effect once as JSON, play it with one line of Java or a command. F3+T reloads it. A generated schema
    gives your editor completion and checking.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Same on every client" icon="random">
    The server picks the seed and parameters. Particles run at a fixed rate from seeded randomness, and players who
    arrive late join effects on entities at the right moment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shaderpacks" icon="sun">
    Kinesis draws after the shaderpack composite, with its own bloom, heat haze and screen effects, so effects look
    right under Oculus with any pack. Model copies that replace an entity are lit and shadowed by the pack.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
